What You'll Need to Fill Out

Every bank asks for the same basics. Have these ready so your application takes a few minutes — you type them only on the bank's official application page.

  • Full Legal Name — matching your government ID
  • Date of Birth & Residential Street Address — a physical address, not just a P.O. box
  • Social Security Number (SSN) or ITIN — the bank uses it to verify your identity and check eligibility
  • Annual Income, Housing Status & Employment Info
  • Identity Proof / Verification Step — a government ID check or a one-time code, if the bank asks
Your application and personal details go only to the bank you choose — never to NexaMatch. We never ask for your password, and we never collect or store sensitive IDs on this site.
